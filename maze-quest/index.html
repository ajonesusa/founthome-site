Tips for great photo mazes

  • Draw with a dark pen or marker on white paper.
  • Take the photo in good light, straight on, with the whole maze in view.
  • Drag the four dots onto the corners of the maze, then tap where it starts and ends.
  • If the app says there's no way through, use the eraser to open the gap. Undo fixes mistakes.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in for careful erasing.

Keeping your mazes

Saved mazes stay on your device and through app updates. Before changing phones or deleting the app, tap Back up at the bottom of the home screen and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Use Restore on the new phone.
