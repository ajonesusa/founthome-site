Maze Quest Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Maze Quest is made by Fount LLC

Short version: Maze Quest does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your photos and mazes stay on your device.

What the app does with your photos

When you photograph a maze, or pick a photo of one, Maze Quest turns it into a maze on your device. The photo is never uploaded anywhere. If you save the maze, a small thumbnail of the photo is kept with it, only on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Maze Quest has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet to send anything about you or your device.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera only so you can photograph a maze, and opens your photo library only when you choose a photo. You can turn these off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups

If you tap Back up, the app creates a file of your saved mazes and opens the standard iPhone share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). Restore reads a backup file you pick.

Children

Maze Quest is made for kids and families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. Backup and restore are behind a grown-up check.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
