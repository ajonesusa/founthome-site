Tips for great photo mazes

  • Draw with a dark pen or marker on white paper.
  • Take the photo in good light, straight on, with the whole maze in view.
  • If the app says there's no way through, use the eraser to open the gap, then tap Next.
  • Back up your saved mazes from the bottom of the home screen before changing phones.
