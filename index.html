About Fount LLC

Fount LLC is a Minnesota limited liability company, founded in 2020. It's an independent, family-run studio that designs and builds mobile apps for iPhone, with a focus on creative play for kids. Maze Quest is our first app. It began with the mazes our own kids draw and wanted to walk through.
